Which approach works best?

Simple Growth Rates

Assumes the future repeats the past at a fixed rate — cannot capture shifts, cycles, or provide confidence ranges

Macro-Driven Model

Uses interest rates, unemployment, and other economic indicators as inputs — but overfits to historical noise, producing unrealistic explosive forecasts, and future Call Report variables are not available

Pure ARIMA ✦

Learns the series' own patterns — trend, momentum, seasonality — and projects them forward with honest uncertainty ranges

Why Pure ARIMA is the right choice
🔄

Self-Contained

No outside forecasts needed — the model relies only on the series' own history to predict its future

📈

Macro Already Baked In

Rate hikes, recessions, and recoveries have already shaped past growth — ARIMA carries those effects forward naturally

⚖️

Stable Across Horizons

Produces realistic paths at 1, 3, and 5 years out — no runaway growth or implausible collapses that plague richer models

📊

Clear Confidence Ranges

Every forecast comes with a range of likely outcomes — narrow at 1 year, wider at 5 years — so decision-makers know exactly how much certainty to place on each horizon
